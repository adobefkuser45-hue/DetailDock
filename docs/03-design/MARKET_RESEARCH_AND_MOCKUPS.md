## 2. Standout Creative Differentiators (The "Attraction & Conversion" Engine)

How DetailDock creates an irresistible user experience that keeps visitors engaged and drives bookings:

1. **The Visual Body-Type Multiplier Engine (Cockpit Selector):**
   - Instead of a dropdown, customers interact with glowing silhouette cards (`Sedan`, `Compact SUV`, `Full SUV / Truck`, `Coupe`). Selecting a truck immediately displays the `1.4x` multiplier badge with an explanation ("Accommodates extended surface area and multi-stage roof de-contamination").
2. **Interactive Before/After Paint Defect Slider & Gloss Depth Meter:**
   - Visual proof of craftsmanship. Users drag an interactive divider comparing heavy swirl marks and scratches against 9H ceramic mirror gloss, accompanied by a dynamic `Gloss Depth Meter (65 GU ➔ 98 GU Ultra High Gloss)`.
3. **The Live Bay Capacity Scheduler (Scarcity & Exclusivity Trigger):**
   - Detailing studios have physical limits (e.g., 2 wash bays). By showing real-time slot status (`Bay 1 Open | Bay 2 Reserved`), customers experience genuine urgency and appreciate the studio's exclusivity.
4. **Authoritative Dynamic Pricing & Time Dial:**
   - As customers toggle add-ons (Engine Bay Steam Clean, Pet Hair Extraction, Leather Ceramic), the estimated price and service duration update with smooth tabular-numeric spring animations.
5. **The Concierge Live Tracking Portal (`DD-XXXXX`):**
   - Post-booking reassurance. Customers track their vehicle's real-time journey:
     `Requested` ➔ `Confirmed` ➔ `In Bay (Decontamination)` ➔ `Paint Inspection` ➔ `Ready for Pickup`.

We have designed and generated 5 high-fidelity visual mockups defining the complete user journey:

### Mockup 2: The Interactive Before/After Paint Correction Showcase

## 4. UI Motion & Interaction Architecture

| Interaction Point | Motion Characteristic | Psychological Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vehicle Type Selection** | Spring scale transition (`stiffness: 450, damping: 35`), subtle electric cyan border glow. | Gives tangible weight to selecting vehicle size. |
| **Price & Duration Dial** | Tabular numeric rolling animation (0 to 60fps). | Eliminates visual layout shifting while conveying high-precision calculation. |
| **Before/After Slider** | Hardware-accelerated clip-path tracking (`transform: translate3d`). | Smooth 1:1 finger and mouse response; highlights craftsmanship. |
| **Booking Confirmation** | Subtle celebratory pulse with high-entropy code generation (`DD-XXXXX`). | High relief and confidence upon order submission. |
| **Kanban Card Drag / Status Click** | Layout spring animation (`layout` prop in Motion). | Fluid, modern administrative experience akin to Linear or Stripe Dashboard. |
